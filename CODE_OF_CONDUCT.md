# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Kodingu to be a place where anyone can learn and contribute, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about the work and respectful to the person.
- Assume good faith, ask before rewriting someone else's contribution, and explain the reason for a change.
- Give and accept specific, actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's code, data, or contact details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not welcome. So is dismissing a report or retaliating against someone who makes one.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern to the maintainer through the contact method on the [eiaiproject GitHub profile](https://github.com/eiaiproject). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a thread, or block an account. They will explain a decision when it is safe to do so.

This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
