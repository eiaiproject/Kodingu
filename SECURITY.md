# Security policy

## Supported version

The live site at <https://kodingu.pages.dev/> is the supported version. The
site is static: it has no account system, backend, or runtime dependency.
Progress, drafts, and preferences stay in the learner's browser.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately through GitHub:

<https://github.com/eiaiproject/Kodingu/security/advisories/new>

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability that could affect other
learners. Include the page or file, the steps to reproduce, and what you
expected to happen.

Reports are reviewed as time allows. There is no bug bounty.
